The faculty supports the faculty members in the areas of lecturing and research by providing a very good infrastructure. Thus, seminar rooms in the institute building can be booked and the faculty council room can be reserved, especially for graduations. The FSZ IT offers support with technical questions. Please find all further information under the corresponding headings.

## Faculty IT Services Office (FSZ IT)

The Faculty Service Centre IT (FSZ IT), part of the Institute for Information Systems, supports the central facilities as well as the institutes and professorships of the Faculty of Economics in the procurement of hardware and software, network configuration, the use of IT services and ensures Internet access. Wi-Fi is available in almost all rooms of the faculty.

The Information Systems Institute also has four complex, high-performance laboratories at its disposal. These include a high-performance computing lab, as well as a virtual reality lab.

The FSZ IT also looks after the information projector in the foyer of the Institute building. The content of the projector advertisements must be approved by the Dean's Office ( [by mail](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/faculty/service-and-it# "E-Mail an das Dekanat der Wirtschaftswissenschaftlichen Fakultät"))

More information can be found on the FSZ IT [homepage](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/institut-fuer-wirtschaftsinformatik/fakultaetsservicezentrum-it).
