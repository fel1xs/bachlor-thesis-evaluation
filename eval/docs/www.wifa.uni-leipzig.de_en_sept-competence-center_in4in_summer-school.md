**Dates:**

- Onboarding Week: June  23rd to 26th (virtual)

_\*\*\*Please note that this Summer School is an exclusive offer to DAAD scholarship holders of the In-Country/In-Region Programme. Further, applying is only possible if you have not yet participated in a (virtual) Summer School from this DAAD programme line.\*\*\*_

**The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD)**, with financial support provided by the Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ), supports academic qualification measures for individuals from developing countries who participate in the In-Country/In-Region Programme. The programme aims to enable trained professionals to address and deal with diverse societal, economic, and ecological issues in developing countries. A further objective is to sustainably improve the teaching and research conditions in the partner regions and to strengthen their higher education profile.

**As an additional offer for scholarship holders of the In-Country/In-Region Programme**, the DAAD cooperates with German universities in organizing a range of summer schools in Germany. One of these is the GREEN IDEA Summer School offered in cooperation with the SEPT Competence Center (Small Enterprise Promotion and Training) located at Leipzig University, Germany.

**The GREEN IDEA Summer School aims to enable you** to plan and implement your projects for knowledge transfer. Therefore, the project ideas are at the heart of the Summer School. We will focus on three channels of knowledge transfer: Innovation, Entrepreneurship (business idea development) and Teaching. Based on the three channels, the Summer School allows you to emphasize one of those tracks by submitting a respective project idea with the application. One intensive Coaching Day per week in Leipzig supports you to advance the project based on the input of the respective week. Each of the tracks aims to empower you to develop your own project idea further as a direct output of the Summer School.

[Check out the alumni video testimonies here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w9WySOSTPt0 "Alumni Video Testimonies")

## Your Benefits

By participating in the GREEN IDEA Summer School, you will profit from a number of personal and academic benefits:

- **Training of a value of 5.000€** including a 3,5-week stay in Leipzig.
- **Workshops and coaching** with experienced partners from Africa and Germany.
- **Discussions and contacts** with international peers.
- Access to **international (academic) networks** via our official community app, during community events such as movie screenings, cooking events, intercultural workshops, and the monthly SEPT Research Colloquium.
- Potential contacts to support you during the **application process** for research stays, etc.
- Get a first glimpse of **German everyday culture** and meet the SEPT Team including students and researchers!
- **Certificate of participation** issued by Leipzig University and DAAD.
- In the Entrepreneurship and Innovation tracks, you will be well-prepared for joining the **AGEA Business Idea Competition**.
- In the Training track, you will have a **well-developed course draft** at hand to be used and published.

## Apply Now!

1\. Read the information on this website carefully and watch the [SEPT tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=noVxrfulyv0 "SEPT tutorial") on German application standards.

2\. Fill in the [online application form](https://umfrage.uni-leipzig.de/index.php/135372?lang=en "Online application form"). Take time to prepare the statements on motivation and experience. The details are required to coordinate the application process with DAAD and the other SP/DL Summer Schools.

3\. Submit your **CV, Letter of Endorsement** and the **IDEA Canvas** to [**septschool(at)uni-leipzig.de**](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/in4in/summer-school# "Email").

Once you have completed those steps you can await the result of our selection process. Please be patient with us, we need some time to do this since we want to treat your application with the careful attention that it deserves.

The selection will be based on the quality of the project idea (innovativeness, feasibility and impact), the expected value addition through SEPT throughout the IDEA Summer School,the credibility of the applicants capacity to implement the project and the sustainability of the project design. Formal quality of the application will also play a role. The selection committee, comprised of high-profile higher education management experts and staff of DAAD, meets in March and the selected candidates will be informed about the results of the selection meeting by the end of April 2026.
