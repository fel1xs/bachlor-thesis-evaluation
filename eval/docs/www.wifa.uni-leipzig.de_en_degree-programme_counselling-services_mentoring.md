On this page you will find all the information you need about our support services for studying at the Faculty of Economics and Management Science. In terms of content, we have combined these topics in the mentoring.

[![enlarge the image: Foto: Notitzheft mit den Worten "Good News"](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/c/2/csm_2016_11_COLOURBOX18219068_good_news_d1f12f0d9f.jpg)](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/ul/Fotos/2016_11_COLOURBOX18219068_good_news.jpg) photo: Colourbox

- Do you have questions about the organisation of your degree programme at the Faculty of Economics and Management Science?
- Are you interested in studying for a Bachelor's or Master's degree programme in an economics-related field at Leipzig University?
- You would like to present your vocational group and your company to the students of our faculty?

You can obtain information, advice and solutions on these and other topics from the Mentoring team of the Faculty of Economics and Management Science:

### [For prospective students](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/counselling-services/mentoring\#collapse389615)

- Structure of the degree programmes (Bachelor and Master)

- Contact person for all questions concerning the degree programmes at the Faculty of Economics and Management Science

- Information on the application procedures at the Faculty

- Admission to study at the Faculty of Economics and Management Science

- Entry/start of studies

- Change of degree programme

- Information event "Study for a day... in the Department of Economics“


### [For students](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/counselling-services/mentoring\#collapse389621)

- Structure of the degree programmes (Bachelor and Master)

- Organisation of studies of the degree programmes in Economics and Management Science (Bachelor and Master)

- Contact person for all questions concerning the degree programmes at the Faculty of Economics and Management Science

- Person of trust for all kinds of problems related to studying at the Faculty of Economics and Management Science

- Semester abroad (path abroad and possibilities of recognition of study achievements abroad)

- Career guidance

- Organisation of the supplementary course in mathematics for first-year students

- Techniques of scientific work

- Change of degree programme/abandoning of studies

- Contact person for exchange students at the Faculty of Economics and Management Science


### [For lecturers](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/counselling-services/mentoring\#collapse389676)

- Organisation and support of the course evaluations
- Organisation of the degree programme evaluations
- Contact person for the supervision of exchange students

### [For graduates / companies](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/counselling-services/mentoring\#collapse389624)

- Contact for companies to promote the career guidance of our students

- Exchange with graduates of the Faculty for (critical) reflection upon the Economics and Management Science degree programme


Do you have questions about other topics? Simply get in touch with us.
