## Frequently Asked Questions

### [When can I start applying? When is it too late to apply?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396296)

A: It is recommended to send the application no more than two months before the deadlines, so all your documents are updated,

- **Self-finance:** 1st January –15th March for tuition beginning in October of the same year
- **DAAD Scholarship**: 1st August –1st October for tuition beginning in October of the following year

For scholarship candidates, consider one year ahead of your desired year entry to study

### [Are there any tips for a successful application?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396334)

A: Yes!

- Submit a complete application at least one month before the application's deadline.
- Write a convincing application essay explaining your experience and expectations and the importance of this MBA in your professional development.
- Upload your documents complete, clear, and clean.

### [Where should I send my application for a DAAD EPOS Scholarship?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396342)

A: If you are a DAAD scholarship candidate, upload your application document and include the [Application Form](https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?origin=46&status=3&subjectGrps=&daad=&q=epos&page=1&detail=50076777#voraussetzungenyte8yuht "Application Form") and the [DAAD checklist](https://static.daad.de/media/daad_de/pdfs_nicht_barrierefrei/in-deutschland-studieren-forschen-lehren/epos_checklist.pdf "DAAD checklist") completed and signed, for the DAAD EPOS scholarship.

### [What is the DAAD EPOS scholarship composed of?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396346)

A: For more information about [what the DAAD scholarship cover](https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?status=&origin=&subjectGrps=&daad=&intention=&q=epos&page=1&detail=50076777 "DAAD scholarship")

### [Who will decide if I could get a DAAD EPOS scholarship or not?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396350)

A: A selection committee composed of representatives from the DAAD, the SEPT Competence Center, and the International Center of Leipzig University.

### [Can I get the admission letter before the application deadline for DAAD EPOS?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396354)

A: No, neither for scholarship applicants nor for self-financed. Since a Committee decides the admission allocation, it is not possible to admit any particular student before the regular process is completed.

### [Can I apply if I have any other business or economics academic background?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396368)

A: Yes. The admissions committee looks for good qualitative skills, leadership, and motivation in learning to develop a small business. You can demonstrate these competencies through various undergraduate majors, professional experience, and extra-curricular activities.

### [How much work experience is required?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396370)

A: Minimum two years of professional experience in SME promotion and training. For self-financed candidates is possible to have internships and cooperative education as work experience.

### [Is a four-year degree required?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396372)

A: Yes. If you have studied in a country where the educational system awards bachelor’s degrees after only three years (e.g., India, Singapore, Australia, etc.), we will require one year of additional study or that it fulfills at least 180 ECTS.

Note: Students from India with a 3-year Bachelor´s degree are eligible to apply for a Master’s degree program if they meet the following requirements. Minimum grade: First division/class 60% and above or A = Very Good and above or a GPA from 3.00 onwards.

### [When can I apply for a visa to Germany?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396374)

A: Accepted applicants will receive the admission letter from the University 3 to 4 months before the MBA Program starts. As soon as you get the letter, you can apply for the visa; we recommend being aware in advance of the procedures and other visa requirements.

### [Is an interview required?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396378)

A: Interviews are part of the selection process. The interviews are done online.

### [Once at the University, can I transfer to another MBA program?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396382)

A: It is not possible at our University. We expect you to be part of the SEPT MBA and remain in the program until you complete it.

### [How can I apply for the SEPT MBA Program?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396386)

A: Please upload your complete application documents in PDF files [here](https://sept-mba.bitrix24.site/ "Please upload your complete application documents in PDF files here").

If your application is shortlisted, we will contact you via email and request hard copy documents. Only in this case, you must send the documents via postal mail.

### [May I contact an alumnus of the Master’s Program?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396388)

A: Yes! Graduated students are one of your best sources of information about the program; if you want to contact our Alumni, please e-mail us [sept(at)uni-leipzig.de](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq# "Email to SEPT")

### [I do not speak any German. May I still apply?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396390)

A: As English is the language of instruction, you don't need to speak any German to apply. However, we recommend an intensive two-month (or longer) German course to make your life in Germany easier.

### [How much should I pay for the study?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/faq\#collapse396392)

A: The tuition fee for the SEPT master program is EUR 6,000 payable in four installments (EUR 1,500) before the beginning of each semester. Please note that books and individual study materials are not included in the fees. Additionally, please consider you have to open a blocked account specified by the embassy to cover your basic living expenses in Germany.
