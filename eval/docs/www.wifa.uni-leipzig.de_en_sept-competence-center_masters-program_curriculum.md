The SEPT MBA in Small and Medium-Sized Enterprise Development is a program dedicated to conveying theoretical knowledge, application-oriented findings and practice-relevant experience with regard to the management, promotion and development of SMEs.

[![enlarge the image: A place to work at University Library, Photo: Leipzig University](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/6/2/csm_Website_MBA_bcf22930a7.png)](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/Fakult%C3%A4t_Wifa/Sept_Center/Dateien/MBA_Bilder/Website_MBA.png) MBA Program, Photo: Leipzig University

## Structure

Our MBA is a four-term course that comprises as follows:

## First Semester (from October to February)

To be attended in Leipzig

### [Module 101: Foundations in Economics Development](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/curriculum\#collapse393462)

- Development Economics
- Reading course on Economics
- Excursion to German companies and Institutions of development cooperation

### [Module 102: Entrepreneurship Management](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/curriculum\#collapse393449)

- Business Model development
- Business plan
- Business simulation game
- Entrepreneurship management
- Presentation techniques

### [Module 103: Development Strategies of SMEs](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/curriculum\#collapse393451)

- Sustainable Development Goals and SMEs in the Global South
- Internationalization of SMEs
- Local and Regional Economic Development
- Innovation Technology
- Information Management in SMEs
- Sustainable Management

## Second Semester (from April to July)

To be attended in Leipzig

### [Module 201\*: Innovation Management in SMEs](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/curriculum\#collapse393465)

- Strategic Innovation Management
- Quality Management
- Tools for Innovation Management
- Innovation Systems

### [Module 202\*: Marketing in SMEs](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/curriculum\#collapse393467)

- Marketing in SMEs
- International Marketing
- Market research

### [Module 203\*: SMEs Finance](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/curriculum\#collapse393469)

- Introduction to financial systems
- Financial instruments for SMEs
- Financial planning

### [Module 204\*: Competence Development in SMEs](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/curriculum\#collapse393471)

- Leadership
- Knowledge management
- Competence development
- Organizational learning

### [Module 205\*\*: Research Methodology and Project Management](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/curriculum\#collapse393474)

- Research Methodology and Research design
- Data analysis and presentation
- Project management

### [Module 206\*: Internship](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/curriculum\#collapse393476)

- Minimum of 6 weeks

## Third Semester (from October to February)

It can be carried out outside Germany

### [Module 301: Research Project](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/curriculum\#collapse393484)

- Field research

## Fourth Semester (from April to September)

To be attended in Leipzig

### [Module 401: SEPT Colloquium](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/curriculum\#collapse393493)

- Business Communication
- SEPT Colloquium
- Advanced data analysis
- Master thesis development

## Note

\\* Elective modules

\\*\\* Compulsory module

Formal training at the University is taught in Modules and assessed by either a written exam, an essay, an oral examination, or project reports.
