Great success for the scientifc journal [Electronic Markets - The International Journal on Networked Business](https://electronicmarkets.org/), which has its Editorial Office at the faculty's [Chair for Application Systems](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/information-systems-institute/professorships/chair-of-application-systems)! The journal impact factors (IF) for 2025 have increased with a two-year IF up from **6.8 to 8.8** and a five-year IF up from **10.0 to 12.2.**

The IF metric is a measure for the number of articles published in the journal from 2024 and 2023 (two-year IF) cited in other scientific journals during 2025 (see [https://doi.org/10.1007/s12525-016-0222-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12525-016-0222-5)). A high IF therefore indicates that the articles in this journal were highly relevant for other researchers. The five-year IF reflects the relevance of the articles over a correspondingly longer period and indicates the sustainable development of a journal.

Both IF represent a very positive positioning of the journal among the renowned international [information systems journals](https://electronicmarkets.org/about-em/rankings). The Editorial Office at [Leipzig University's](https://www.uni-leipzig.de/en) [Information Systems Institute](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/information-systems-institute) would like to take this opportunity to thank all reviewers, editorial board members and editors who have contributed to this development!
