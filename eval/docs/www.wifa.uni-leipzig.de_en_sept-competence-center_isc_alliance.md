In close cooperation with our fellow Institute of Business Education and Management Training we want to explore the potential of international students from Leipzig University for the regional job market.

[![enlarge the image: Alliance for skilled Professionals (Fachkräfteallianz), Picture: FIT4export](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/c/e/csm_Fachkr%C3%A4fteallianz_2022_2b4e17cf7a.jpg)](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/Fakult%C3%A4t_Wifa/Sept_Center/Dateien/FIT4export_Bilder/Fachkr%C3%A4fteallianz_2022.jpg) Alliance for skilled Professionals (Fachkräfteallianz), Picture: FIT4export

The goal of the project is to offer you – the international students in Leipzig - interesting, challenging and fun activities to connect with the regional job market. Our main goal is to improve the personal and business-related networks of foreign students and alumni to become skilled professionals for the region, in our case, with a focus on business internationalisation.

[![enlarge the image: Alliance for skilled Professionals (Fachkräfteallianz), Picture: FIT4export](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/a/b/csm_Fachkr%C3%A4fteallianz_2022_1_b79bba9d7e.jpg)](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/Fakult%C3%A4t_Wifa/Sept_Center/Dateien/FIT4export_Bilder/Fachkr%C3%A4fteallianz_2022_1.jpg) Alliance for skilled Professionals (Fachkräfteallianz), Picture: FIT4export

International students are welcome to join our activities and workshops to boost their career opportunities in our region – so check out the offers below and contact us if you have any questions:

- The [Online Marketing Challenge](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/fit4export/omc-2022/ "Online Marketing Challenge") is a unique opportunity for students to gain practical experience in the real world while creating and executing digital marketing campaigns for real Saxon companies working in different industries using a budget of 500 Euros.
- Qualification of academic skilled and management professionals for the regional workplace: [Gear Up your Career](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/institut-fuer-wirtschaftspaedagogik/forschung/fachkraefteallianz/ "Gear Up your Career")
- Integration of international students into the local job market through market research projects integrated in our [SEPT MBA program](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/ "SEPT MBA program")
- Other training sessions arranged by the SEPT Competence Center

\- [Export Management Advanced Training](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/fit4export/training/ "Export Management Advanced Training")

\- [Innovation Management](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/in4in/training/ "Innovation Management")
