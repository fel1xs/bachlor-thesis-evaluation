## information security

The processing of information is an indispensable part of almost all processes at Leipzig University. Potential security incidents at Leipzig University must be prevented by taking appropriate countermeasures at all levels. In addition to implementing protective measures to ensure the proper functioning of information technology, this also includes protecting non-electronically processed information through appropriate security processes.

All faculties and institutions at Leipzig University appoint a contact person for information security who works closely with the University of Leipzig's information security officer on security-related issues to ensure the appropriate availability, confidentiality and integrity of information within the scope of official requirements.
