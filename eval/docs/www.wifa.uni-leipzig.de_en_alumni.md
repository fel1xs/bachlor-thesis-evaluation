Dear alumni of the Faculty of Economics and Management Science, even after you have left us, we are very happy to remain in touch with you.

[![enlarge the image: Studierende auf dem Leibnizforum](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/7/4/csm_2018_Leibnizforum_OO3A4475_5fb8acdda7.jpg)](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/ul/Fotos/2018_Leibnizforum_OO3A4475_UL_web.jpg) Student life at the Leibniz Forum. Photo: Swen Reichold

Here you can sign up for our alumni mailing list and receive information about faculty events in the future. In particular, we would like to draw your attention to interesting events to which you are cordially invited in order to learn interesting facts, exchange viewpoints, maintain contacts and establish new ones.

Your Dean's Office and Study Office.

Of course, you can also unsubscribe from the mailing list at any time. To do so, simply contact the Dean's Office ( [by mail](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/alumni# "E-Mail an dekanat[at]wifa.uni-leipzig.de")).
