News from  02/07/2026

On Friday, 26 June 2026, Leipzig University’s International SEPT MBA (Small Enterprise Promotion and Training) celebrated the graduation of its Class of 2024–26 in the historic Alter Senatssaal.

[![Picture: SEPT](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/b/9/csm_02.07.2026_2_cb2af89a56.jpg)Picture: SEPT](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/b/9/csm_02.07.2026_2_1d7a7b3a33.jpg)

**The ceremony marked the successful completion** of two years of intensive study for 18 graduates representing 15 countries, including Ghana, Pakistan, Bolivia, Armenia, and Egypt. Despite the summer heat, the room was filled with graduates, their families and friends, university staff, and distinguished guests to celebrate this important milestone.

**Opening the ceremony, SEPT Director Prof. Dr. Utz Dornberger** reflected on the rapidly changing global environment shaped by digitalization, artificial intelligence, and ongoing geopolitical and economic challenges. In this context, he emphasized the importance of international collaboration and lifelong professional networks, encouraging graduates to remain active members of both the global SEPT community and Leipzig University’s alumni network.

One of the ceremony’s longstanding traditions was the **presentation of the Best Business Plan Award**. This year’s award was presented to Sardor Ikramov from Uzbekistan in recognition of his outstanding entrepreneurial concept. On behalf of the graduating class, Lissette Adriana Salavarría Gómez from Ecuador reflected on the shared experiences of studying abroad, the friendships formed across cultures, and the resilience that had guided the cohort through the past two years.

**The International SEPT MBA** prepares professionals from around the world to promote entrepreneurship and strengthen small and medium-sized enterprises as drivers of sustainable economic development. By combining academic excellence with practical experience and an international perspective, the programme equips its graduates to contribute to innovation, inclusive growth, and positive change in their home countries and beyond.

**The entire SEPT team warmly congratulates the Class of 2024–26 and wishes its graduates every success in their future careers. We look forward to seeing the impact they will make as members of the global SEPT alumni network.**
