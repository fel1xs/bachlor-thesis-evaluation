## International Business & Export Management Advanced Training (EMAT)

## About EMAT

In times of an internationalizing world economy, small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and startups have more opportunities to conquer new markets. Moreover, the level of innovation, compliance with product parameters, and the high-quality products of German enterprises are often unrivaled. To leverage this advantage over often significantly larger competitors, SMEs and startups require top personnel among export and sales managers specialized in international business.

In response to this demand, the International Business and Export Management Advanced Training (EMAT) aims to expand and refine existing export management knowledge by developing skills in areas such as market and customer analysis, export planning and management, international negotiation skills, intercultural competencies, export financing, customs issues, among others.

**The EMAT training is open to international students and alumni living in Saxony**, as well as to **professionals from SMEs and startups**.

## Learning by Doing

To ensure the practical relevance of the project, we bring together innovative German SMEs and start-ups with international students and alumni. The SME or start-up provides a business case in the areas of international business development, trade, or export management, which the participant group uses as a case study during the practical part of the program. Participants gradually conduct market research for the target country and develop market entry recommendations.

During the project, visits to the partner company’s facilities may be arranged.

Professionals taking part in the training may also work on their own business cases.

## A Way to the Job Market

Working with SMEs and startups is not only about ensuring the practical dimension of learning but also about getting to know potential future employers.

International students and alumni participating in the EMAT project can register for an A2 German language course to improve their German and increase their chances in the job market. The course will take place in Leipzig. More details about this offer you can find in the language course flyer.

## Duration

The project kicks off with an opening session on **September 25, 2026**. During this session, the participating companies will present their profiles, product or service portfolios, current international activities, goals for international expansion, and initial ideas for the scope of the practical project.

**Training sessions** will be held every **Friday (3:00 PM – 6:15 PM)** and **Saturday (9:00 AM – 12:15 PM or 9:00 AM – 4:30 PM)**, starting on **October 9, 2026**.

A **free German A2 language course** will be offered in Leipzig by the Studienkolleg Sachsen at Leipzig University and will run from **October 2026 to February 2027**. _Please note that the number of places is limited!_

Upon successful completion and presentation of their practical projects, EMAT participants will receive a **Certificate of Participation** from **Leipzig University** and the **SEPT Competence Center**, co-signed by the partner company.

## Information

### [Benefits](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/isc/training\#collapse829939)

**Students get:**

- Practical experience with local SMEs and startups
- A Bootcamp on Export Management with experts from the field
- Support from experienced trainers and coaches
- Contacts with potential future employers
- Free A2 German language course
- A certificate issued by Leipzig University

**Companies get:**

- A Bootcamp on Export Management
- Tailored market research and entry recommendations for the selected target country
- Knowledge about the target market
- Contacts with potential business partners abroad
- Contact with skilled international staff

### [Content](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/isc/training\#collapse609628)

**Part I: International Business Model Development**

- International business model canvas
- Strategic market and competitors analysis
- Customer analysis and planning, value proposition
- Market entry and international distribution strategy

**Part II: Market Entry Plan**

- Role of culture in international business
- International negotiation & influencing
- Payment and delivery conditions – Incoterms 2020
- Duty and foreign trade - customs law and export control
- Financing foreign trade - overview
- Formation of international contracts with examples
- Guarantees in foreign trade - introduction to Letter of Credit

**Part III: Project Coaching**

- Export plan for product/service
- Market entry plan development for SME/startup
