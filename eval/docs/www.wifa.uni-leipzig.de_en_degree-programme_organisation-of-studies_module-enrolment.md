## Module Enrolment - How, Where and When?

### [How, where and when do I register for the modules of the faculty?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/organisation-of-studies/module-enrolment\#collapse401995)

The registration for the modules of the Faculty of Economics and Management Science via the [TOOL](https://tool.uni-leipzig.de/einschreibung/login/login "To the TOOL (link opens in a new tab)") platform commences in the summer semester of 2026 on **Tuesday, 24 March 2026, 12:00 noon and ends on Monday, 30 March 2026, 5:00 pm.**

In TOOL you will be guided step by step and with help texts through the module enrolment.

- The **compulsory modules** are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis (i.e. you are awarded your place in the module immediately) There are a sufficient number of places available for students of the Faculty of Economics and Management Science.
- The **elective compulsory** and **elective modules** of the Faculty of Economics and Management Science are allocated via an allocation procedure, taking into account the priorities set. This means that the module places are only distributed following the enrolment period.
- During the enrolment period, you can change or delete the modules you have been assigned. If you remove the tick from a module that has already been set, you deselect the module again.

The **Module and event registration** also **simultaneously** constitutes the **examination registration**. Attendance alone in a module or course does not constitute entitlement to take the module examination.

If you have already taken a module once and have not yet completed it (due to illness, failed exams or an absence-5), you will not be able to register for a module:

- **Case of illness**: You will be automatically registered by the Study Office for the next possible examination date.
- **Case of failed examination/Absence-5**: You register independently in [AlmaWeb](https://almaweb.uni-leipzig.de/ "To the AlmaWeb (link opens in new tab)") for the resit examination in the period from 18 April 2026 (08 a.m.) until 16 Mai 2026 (11:59 p.m.).

Please observe the further information on resit examinations and corresponding deadlines.

[Find out more](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/organisation-of-studies/examination-matters "zu den Prüfungsangelegenheiten der Wirtschaftswissenschaftlichen Fakultät (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)")

All information on the procedure can be found in the [central instructions for enrolment in the modules of the inter-faculty key qualifications](https://www.uni-leipzig.de/studium/im-studium/moduleinschreibung/#collapse386466 "To the central notices on module enrolment for the SQ modules (link opens in a new tab)").

All the KQ modules as well as the additional notices can be found in the [Course catalogue](https://almaweb.uni-leipzig.de/scripts/mgrqispi.dll?APPNAME=CampusNet&PRGNAME=EXTERNALPAGES&ARGUMENTS=-N000000000000001,-N000405,-Acc "To the course catalogue (link opens in new tab)") in the menu item inter-faculty key qualifications (KQ) in the respective semester.

The taking of inter-faculty key qualifications is only intended for Bachelor students.

### Pro-cooperation with the Leipzig University of Applied Sciences (HTWK)

#### Modules from the field of IT

In the summer semester 2026, students of the Master's degree programme in Business Information Systems can participate in modules of the HTWK from the field of Computer Science.

All modules offered as well as further information can be found in the education portal [OPAL](https://bildungsportal.sachsen.de/opal/auth/RepositoryEntry/9070968838?1 "To the OPAL of the HTWK Leipzig (link opens in a new tab)") of the HTWK. Use your [Uni-Login](https://www.urz.uni-leipzig.de/dienste/uni-login/#c216 "Information on the university login (link opens in a new tab)") for this. Please observe the separate enrolment group for Information Systems and please use the waiting list function if required, as additional places may be released subsequently.

Students of Information Systems can include HTWK modules with a maximum of 10 credit points in the elective area. In total, a maximum of 20 credit points can be credited with modules from the HTWK in the elective area.

**After successful registration for the module, you must enrol for a parallel degree programme at the HTWK.**

Enrolment in the parallel study programme at the HTWK takes place upon application to the [Department of Student Affairs at the HTWK](https://www.htwk-leipzig.de/hochschule/organisation-struktur/dezernate/dezernat-studienangelegenheiten/ "To the Department of Student Affairs of the HTWK (link opens in a new tab)"). Students of the Master's degree programmes in Management Science and Information Systems enrol for the first semester of the Master's degree programme in Management Science at the HTWK. Proof of the semester fee already paid at the Leipzig University must be provided by submitting a current certificate of enrolment from Leipzig University. The deadline for submitting the application and the certificate of enrolment to the Department of Student Affairs of the HTWK is **31 March** for the summer semester.

If you **repeatedly participate** in modules at HTWK Leipzig, you do not have to register again for the parallel study programme. In this case, a current certificate of enrolment from Leipzig University is sufficient for re-registration, which must be submitted to the Department of Student Affairs at the HTWK by 31 March 2026 at the latest with the note "ProKooperation".

### [Is there a procedure for late registration?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/organisation-of-studies/module-enrolment\#collapse383013)

For a late registration for the modules of the Faculty of Economic and Management Sciences, please register directly in the [Late Registration Portal](https://fszitapp.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/NRV "To the late registration portal (link opens in new tab)").

(Please note: Registration in the Late Registration Portal is merely an expression of interest in a module place. It is not a guarantee of obtaining a place on the module. The decision on the allocation of succession places is made by the professors responsible for the module).

Please note that late registration for the modules of the Faculty of Economic and Management Sciences is **only** possible via the succession portal.

The Late Registration Portal opens at **9 a.m. on the first day of lectures** each semester.

Modules for which there is no advanced procedure are also listed. If your desired module is not displayed at all in the portal, please check in the course catalogue whether it is offered at all and then please enquire at the professorship offering the module.

**Please observe the following notices:**

- Master modules may only be taken by Master's students.
- In the modules **Mathematics** **for Economics and Management Science II** and **Commercial and company law for economists,** only students in whose degree programme these modules are compulsory can obtain a place here.
- If you **have to repeat** an exam in a module because you were absent for the first exam due to illness or without excuse, or if you failed it, **you are still registered** for that module. This means that you are **not allowed** to register for this module in the **late registration procedure**.

### [I have to repeat an exam. How can I take the module again?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/organisation-of-studies/module-enrolment\#collapse384889)

If you have to repeat an exam in a module because you were absent for the first exam due to illness or without excuse, or if you failed it, **you are still registered** for that module. This means that you **cannot register for it again in TOOL**. You are also not allowed to register for this module in the late registration procedure. Please enquire with the professorship offering the module whether and how you **can participate in the courses of the module again**.

**Do not forget to register for the repeat examination in good time!**

- You were **ill** for the examination → **the Study Office** automatically registers you for the next possible examination date.
- You have **failed** the exam or received an Absence 5 notice → You must register independently in [**AlmaWeb**](https://almaweb.uni-leipzig.de/ "To the AlmaWeb (link opens in new tab)") for the resit exam in the period from 18 April 2026 (8 a.m.) to 16 May 2026 (11:59 p.m.).

Please observe the further information on resit examinations and corresponding deadlines.

Further details on registering for or deregistering from examinations in the first/second or third attempt can be found under [Examination Matters](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/organisation-of-studies/examination-matters).
