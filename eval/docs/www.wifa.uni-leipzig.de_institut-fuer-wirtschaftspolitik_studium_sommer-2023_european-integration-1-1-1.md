## Course description

This course aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of the economic and political foundations of the European integration process. In the first part, we will explore the history and underlying rationale of European integration, applying fundamental economic concepts to assess the impact of EU trade policy, regional policy, factor market integration, and agricultural policy. The second part will focus on the creation of the European Monetary Union, examining its implications, shortcomings, and the challenges associated with its enlargement. Topics include (but are not limited to):

1\. History of European integration

2\. European institutions and decision making

3\. Microeconomics of trade and tariffs and preferential trade liberalization

4\. Market size and scale effects

5\. Common agricultural policy

6\. Optimum currency area theory and the European Monetary Union

## Course objectives

By the end of the course, students will be able to:

- Understand the economic rationale behind European integration
- Analyze trade integration and its welfare effects
- Explain the functioning and challenges of the Eurozone
- Understand EU institutions and policy-making processes
- Critically assess current issues (e.g., crises, enlargement, Brexit)
