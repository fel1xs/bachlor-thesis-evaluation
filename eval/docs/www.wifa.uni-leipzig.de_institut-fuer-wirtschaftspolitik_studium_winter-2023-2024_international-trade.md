## References

For deeper understanding of the contents of the lecture notice the reading list. Students are encouraged to follow the ongoing debate on international trade as reported in the [Economist](http://www.economist.com/), [Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung](http://www.faz.net/) and [Financial Times](http://www.ft.com/home/uk), [Reuters](http://de.reuters.com/), [Bloomberg](http://www.bloomberg.com/), or the news on the [WTO website](http://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news_e.htm).
