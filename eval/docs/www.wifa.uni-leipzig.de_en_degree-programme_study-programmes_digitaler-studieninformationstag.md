Every year in spring, we welcome our prospective students to the University Information Day on campus. We cordially invite you to get to know the Faculty of Economics and Management Science and our degree programmes and to get a taste of campus life.

[![enlarge the image: Students walk across the campus, photo: Christian Hüller](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/b/4/csm_SIT_Studierende-laufen-%C3%BCber-Campus_2_c5d18b2746.jpg)](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/b/4/csm_SIT_Studierende-laufen-%C3%BCber-Campus_2_b286053cc9.jpg) Students walk across the campus, photo: Christian Hüller

We, the Faculty of Economics and Management Science, will be presenting the following at the University Information Day:

- our Bachelor's degree programmes in Economics and Management Science and Business Information Systems,
- our Teaching profession for vocational schools subject area Business and Administration,
- our Master´s programmes in Management Science, Economics, Information Systems, Business Education & Management Training and Quantitative Economics for Sustainability,
- the reasons for studying at our faculty, and
- the opportunities for further counselling and information.

The University Information Day will take place on **Saturday, May the 9th 2026 from 11.00 am to approx. 3.00 pm**. We have planned the following programme for the day:

- 11.00 am to 12.00 am Information event **Overview of the study and the degree programmes at the Faculty of Economics and Manageement Science (Bachelor's degree and Teaching profession for vocational schools subject area Business and Administration)** in the Institute Building Seminar Room 2.
- 12.00 am to 1.30 pm Advising sessions for **Bachelor Economics and Management Science** (Faculty Council Room) and **Bachelor Business Information Systems** (Room IZ 09); both in the Institute building
- 12.30 am to 1.30 pm Information event **Presentation of the Master's programmes of the Faculty of Economics and Management Science** in the Institute building Seminar room 2
- 1.30 pm to 3.00 pm Advising sessions for **Master Management Science** (Faculty Council Room), **Master Economics** and **Quantitative Economics for Sustainability** (Seminar room 2), **Master Information Systems** (Room IZ 09) and **Master Business Education & Management Training** (Room IZ 27); all in the Institute building

We are happy to welcome you at our Faculty.
