The Master's programme comprises four semesters (two years) and concludes with a Master of Science. The focus of the degree programme is on the business learning processes, which you can enhance individually with a specialisation in business administration and economics, business informatics or sustainability management. The degree programme prepares you for responsible and versatile positions in companies, in public administration or in research. On the following page you can find out more about the programme content, the course of study and the application process.

## Master of Science in Business Education and Management Training

Building on a Bachelor's degree with a focus on Business and Economics, the degree programme teaches advanced business education skills and knowledge. The degree programme enables graduates to take on responsible, pedagogically accentuated specialist and management tasks in companies, in vocational education and training institutions and in research institutions. The programme focuses on the dynamic structure of vocational education and training at the interface between business and education. You will deal with the central content of vocational and business education and can specialise individually in the following related disciplines:

- Business Administration
- Economics
- Business Informatics
- Sustainability Management

Instead of specialising, you can also take the modules freely according to your interests.

### [Structure of the degree programme](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/study-programmes/masters-degree-in-business-education-and-management-training\#collapse389140)

[![enlarge the image: Structure of the degree programme](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/b/b/csm_WiP%C3%A4d_MSc_en_a94f755ef9.jpg)](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/b/b/csm_WiP%C3%A4d_MSc_en_0fe78a406f.jpg) Structure of the degree programme

The Master’s programme in Business Education (M.Sc.) is structured in modules and comprises a total of 120 credit points (CP). It includes compulsory modules in business education worth 30 CP, compulsory elective modules worth a total of 40 CP, an elective module area worth 30 CP, and a Master’s thesis worth 20 CP.

The compulsory elective areas are divided as follows:

- Compulsory Elective Area 1 (30 CP) is designed for specialisation. Here, you can choose from a wide range of modules in business administration, economics, business informatics, and sustainability management.
- Compulsory Elective Area 2 (10 CP) is a compulsory elective area in business education. One of three business education modules must be taken as a compulsory module.

The free elective area (30 CP) allows students to take any original Master’s modules (modules at Master’s level) offered by the University of Leipzig. The recommended study schedule, including an overview of the compulsory and compulsory elective modules, can be downloaded [here](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/Fakult%C3%A4t_Wifa/Fakult%C3%A4t/Studienb%C3%BCro/2_Studienorganisation/1_Studiengangsaufbau/2026-05_Studienablaufplan_und_Modul%C3%BCbersicht_Master_WiP%C3%A4d.pdf "To the study schedule (Link opens in a new tab)").

Depending on the modules taken in Compulsory Elective Area 1 and the topic of the Master’s thesis, students have the option of having a specialisation indicated on their Master’s degree certificate. Possible specialisations include Business Administration, Economics, Business Informatics or Sustainability Management.
