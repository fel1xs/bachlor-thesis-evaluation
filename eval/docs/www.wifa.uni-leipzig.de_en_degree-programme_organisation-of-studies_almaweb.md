AlmaWeb stands for the campus management system of the Alma Mater Lipsiensis - Leipzig University. The functionalities system for students and teachers are explained.

[![enlarge the image: ](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/2/2/csm_Almaweb_Logo_gross_cca00ae2c5.png)](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/2/2/csm_Almaweb_Logo_gross_e69503240a.png) AlmaWeb is Leipzig University’s central study portal.

## Functionalities of the study portal

The IT system AlmaWeb supports the organisation of studies and teaching at Leipzig University both technically and structurally.

### [What can I deal with as a student in AlmaWeb?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/organisation-of-studies/almaweb\#collapse412039)

For students, AlmaWeb offers access to all important/student functions via a [Web portal](https://almaweb.uni-leipzig.de/ "To the AlmaWeb web portal (link opens in new tab)") such as:

- Application for study places and admission,
- Course catalogue,
- Summary of the [Modules](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/studium/studienorganisation/moduleinschreibung.html "Information on module enrolment at the Faculty of Economics and Management Science") attended,
- [Overview of grades](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/organisation-of-studies/examination-matters#c422283 "How do I obtain an official grade overview? (Link opens in a new tab)") and [exam registration](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/organisation-of-studies/examination-matters "To the examination matters"),
- [Thesis upload](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/organisation-of-studies/examination-matters "To the examination matters"),
- Feedback and change of address,
- Access to the news portal.

Please ensure that the personal data stored there is correct and up-to-date. After a move you should, for instance, enter the new address in the system as soon as possible. Forwarding messages sent via AlmaWeb to your university email address is also very worthwhile. From the university email address, you can in turn set up a forwarding function to your private email address.

### [Which functionalities does AlmaWeb offer me as a lecturer?](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/organisation-of-studies/almaweb\#collapse412042)

The entire student administration of both the Bachelor's degree programmes and the Master's degree programmes of the Faculty of Economics and Management Science is performed centrally via the campus management system AlmaWeb.

For lecturers AlmaWeb offers access via a [Web portal](https://almaweb.uni-leipzig.de/ "To the AlmaWeb web portal (link opens in new tab)") to all important functions for the administration of courses and examinations, such as:

- Overview of own courses with a list of dates,
- Insight into the constantly updated lists of course participants,
- Option to contact registered students (just courses),
- Provision of course materials for registered students,
- Overview of the registered examination participants,
- direct entry of grades,
- Download electronically submitted theses.

The individual functions are presented in more detail in the [**Click guide for lecturers**](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/Fakult%C3%A4t_Wifa/Fakult%C3%A4t/Studienb%C3%BCro/2_Studienorganisation/3_Moduleinschreibung/Webportal_Lehrkraefte_Anleitung.pdf "To the AlmaWeb guide for lecturers(file link opens in a new tab)"). The process for downloading the theses is described in a separate [**Click guide (theses)**](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/Fakult%C3%A4t_Wifa/Fakult%C3%A4t/Studienb%C3%BCro/2_Studienorganisation/4_Pr%C3%BCfungsangelegenheiten/Klickanleitung_Elektronisches_Einreichen_Abschlussarbeiten_Lehrende.pdf "flielink opens in a new tab"). Registered lecturers automatically have access to all these functions. If necessary, deputies (for example for secretariats or academic staff) can also be set up on request.
