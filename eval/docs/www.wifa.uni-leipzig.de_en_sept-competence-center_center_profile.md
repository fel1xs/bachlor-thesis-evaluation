The SEPT Competence Center is a research and training center at the Faculty of Economics and Management Science of Leipzig University dedicated to providing theoretical insight as well as practical experience in the management and promotion of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) worldwide.

[![enlarge the image: Central Campus at Leipzig University. Photo: Leipzig University](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/1/6/csm_Center-Logo_62856c8d19.png)](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/Fakult%C3%A4t_Wifa/Sept_Center/Dateien/Center_Bilder/Center-Logo.png) SEPT Competence Center, Photo: Leipzig University

## About

Because of location in Germany, we have permanently the opportunity to learn from successful German SMEs and to analyze in detail their best practices and winning experiences, as well as those of the institutions that foster and promote them.

These exceptional _'Mittelstand'_ Firms, as they are labeled in Germany, are known worldwide for their dynamism and constant innovation. Additionally, many of them have managed to achieve leadership positions in many different countries and markets, basically with high-value-added products and services.

For these reasons, all programs that are offered by SEPT are based on the patterns of the outstanding German _'Mittelstand'_ and the measures that have been successfuly applied to promote them. Our main goal is to transmit this knowledge to institutions and firms from all over the world that are looking forward to implementing successful strategies in the promotion of SMEs.

All activities of the SEPT Competence Center revolve around SMEs, as it can be seen in the following overview of activities.

## Activities

### [MBA Program in SME Development](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/center/profile\#collapse392700)

**The MBA Program in SME Development** is a practice oriented postgraduate program. The aim of the MBA Program is to convey theoretical knowledge, as well as application-oriented findings and practice-relevant experience surrounding the promotion and development of SMEs. [\[](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program/masters-program "MBA Program in SME Development") [learn more...](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/masters-program "MBA Program in SME Development")\]

### [Research Program](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/center/profile\#collapse392702)

**Our research activities** focus on International Entrepreneurship, Innovation, and University Business Linkages. The research activities carried out within the SEPT Competence center are conducted by an interdisciplinary team of scientists, PhD and master's students with background in economics, social and natural sciences. [\[](https://www.sept.uni-leipzig.de/phd-research/phd-program/ "Opens internal link in new window") [learn more...](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/research "Research and PHD Program")\]

### [SMILE Entrepreneurship Promotion](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/center/profile\#collapse392706)

**In the frame of our SMILE start-up initiative** at Leipzig University, we promote entrepreneurial thinking and action among students, academics and alumni from the Leipzig region. The initiative is continuously developed in close cooperation with strong partners from science and practice [\[](https://www.sept.uni-leipzig.de/selfmanagementinitiativeleipzig/ "Opens internal link in new window") learn more...\]

### [iN4iN Intelligence For Innovation](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/center/profile\#collapse392708)

**iN4iN Intelligence For Innovation** offers specialized training and consultancy services for SMEs and institutions worldwide in the fields of innovation management, business incubator development and the strengthening of university-business linkages. [\[learn more...](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/in4in)\]

### [ISC Internationalization](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/center/profile\#collapse392869)

**Internationalization is both a challenge and an opportunity** for SME. The International Startup Campus (ISC) program offers advanced trainings, coaching and consulting for firms who are interested in exploring foreign markets. [\[](https://www.sept.uni-leipzig.de/fit4export/ "Opens internal link in new window") [learn more...](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/isc "FIT4export Internationalization")\]
