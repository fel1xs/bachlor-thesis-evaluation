In order to gain an impression of the questions and topics that young researchers at our faculty deal with and have dealt with, you will find an overview of the habilitation programmes completed at our institution here. Habilitation theses are available from the Leipzig University Library.

If you wish to apply for a habilitation programme at our Faculty, please apply for entry in the habilitation list after reading the habilitation regulations and the statutes of the Leipzig University on good scientific practice.

If you have any questions, please contact the Dean’s Office Councillor, Dr Martina Diesener.
