## Center

The International SEPT Competence Center is a research and training center at the Faculty of Economics and Management Science of Leipzig University dedicated to provide theoretical insight as well as practical experience in the management and promotion of Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (SMEs) worldwide.

[“Center”](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/center)
