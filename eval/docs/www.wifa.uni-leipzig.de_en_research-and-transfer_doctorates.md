You are interested in doing a doctorate at the Faculty of Economics and Management Science? Here you will find an overview of the possibilities, funding and more.

In principle, it is possible to embark on a doctorate in all of our [subject areas](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/faculty/departments-and-institutes "Zu den Fachbereichen"). Contact the professor directly with your research proposal or enquire at the chairs about the topic on which you could do your doctorate with us.

If you have questions of a general nature, please contact the Dean's Office first. Our staff will be happy to advise you and also help you find the best thematic and professional support.

Once your decision has been made and you have found a supervisor for your thesis, please apply to be included in the Faculty's list of doctoral students. Please also read the doctoral regulations of the Faculty of Economics and Management Science.

We would like to expressly draw your attention to our doctoral programmes, which are explained in more detail below.

On the university's website you will also find information about a potential grant from the State Graduate Funding Scheme and other funding opportunities.

## Doctoral programmes

### [Central-German Doctoral Program Economics (CGDE)](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/research-and-transfer/doctorates\#collapse368059)

The Central-German Doctoral Program Economics (CGDE) is a regionally organised doctoral programme in economics.

Method and research-oriented courses held by internationally outstanding academics form the core of the programme. The CGDE network is supported by the following economics faculties and research institutions in Saxony, Saxony-Anhalt and Thuringia:

- [Faculty of Business and Economics](https://tu-dresden.de/bu/wirtschaft?set_language=en "Zur Seite der Tu Dresden (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)")  (TU Dresden)

- [Ifo Institute for Economic Research Dresden](https://www.ifo.de/en/research/ifo-dresden "Zum ifo Dresden (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)") (ifo Dresden)

- [Faculty of Law, Economics and Business](https://www.jurawiwi.uni-halle.de/?lang=en "Zur Seite der Uni Halle (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)") (Martin Luther University Halle-Wittenberg)
- [Halle Institute for Economic Research – Member of the Leibniz Association](http://www.iwh-halle.de/en "Zum IWH (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)") (IWH)
- [Faculty of Economics and Business Administration](https://www.wiwi.uni-jena.de/en/ "Zur FSU (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)") (Friedrich Schiller University Jena (FSU))
- [Faculty of Economics and Management Science](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/start2.html "Zur Uni Leipzig (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)") (Leipzig University)
- [Faculty of Economics and Management](http://www.fww.ovgu.de/fww/en/Faculty+of+Economics+and+Management.html "Zur Uni Magdeburg (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)") (Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg)

The doctoral programme consists of two areas: the Core Programme consists of courses from the fields of "Frontiers in Macroeconomics", "Frontiers in Microeconomics" and "Frontiers in Econometrics". Each of these courses is organised as an intensive, three-day block event. These events are supplemented by courses offered by internationally recognised researchers who provide insights into their respective fields of research ("Special Courses").

[CGDE-Homepage](http://cgde.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/ "Zum CGDE (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)")

### [Saxon Doctoral Program in Management Research](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/research-and-transfer/doctorates\#collapse368055)

Since 2020, the Saxon Doctoral Programme in Management Research has existed in **business management** and **information systems**, in which all four economics and management science faculties of the universities of Saxony are involved. Method-oriented courses are offered by recognised, internationally renowned researchers.

[Saxon Doctoral Program in Management Research](https://saxondoctoralprogram.de/ "Zur Homepage des Saxon Doctoral Program (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)")

**Workshops for doctoral researchers and early postdocs**

The [Graduate Academy Leipzig](https://www.ga.uni-leipzig.de/en?mtm_campaign=ga&mtm_kwd=mailing), as the central institution for doctoral researchers and early postdocs at Leipzig University, will once again be offering [workshops and training courses](https://www.ga.uni-leipzig.de/en/graduate-academy-leipzig/workshops-and-events?mtm_campaign=workshop&mtm_kwd=mailing) for all disciplines starting in the coming winter semester. The programme includes both online and face-to-face courses on topics such as artificial intelligence, academic writing, career development, research integrity and mental health. It also offers counselling and tailor-made workshops for groups. [Membership](https://www.ga.uni-leipzig.de/en/graduate-academy-leipzig/membership?mtm_campaign=mitgliedschaft&mtm_kwd=mailing) of the Graduate Academy Leipzig is required to take advantage of these offers.

**Become a science slammer**

Have you always wanted to present your research to a wider audience? You will have the chance to do so at the [Science Slam](https://www.ga.uni-leipzig.de/en/graduate-academy-leipzig/workshops-and-events/science-slam-curtain-up-for-science?mtm_campaign=science-slam&mtm_kwd=mailing) on Doctoral Researchers' Day on 12 November at the Grassi Museum - apply now with your doctoral or research topic to become a science slammer! A workshop with the slam's moderator, Franziska Wilhelm, will be held to help you prepare.
