During the last years the SEPT Competence Center at Leipzig University has carried out a lot of conferences, training and workshop events in most of the crucial areas of the small enterprises development and innovation management.

The scientific events and professional training acitivities of the SEPT Competence Center focus on dissemination and application of state-of-the-art methods and expertise surrounding the promotion and development of Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs). Please find below our main events of the past years.
