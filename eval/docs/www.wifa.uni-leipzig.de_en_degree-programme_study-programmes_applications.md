The first section is for those interested in our Bachelor degree programmes and the second section is for those interested in our Master degree programmes.

## Enrolment in a Bachelor's degree programme or teaching degree subject area business and administration

Are you interested in applying for one of our Bachelor's degree programmes or the teaching degree subject area business and administration? Depending on the type of **H** igher **E** ducation **E** ntrance **Q** ualification (German or foreign HEEQ) and the intended enrolment in a specific semester (application for the first semester or a higher semester), there are different application paths.

### [Application for the first semester with a German HEEQ](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/study-programmes/applications\#collapse398624)

This option applies to prospective students who have a **German higher education entrance qualification** (usually a German Abitur) and have not yet taken examinations in a similar Bachelor's degree programme.

It is only possible to make applications for the winter semester. Applications for our **Bachelor's degree programmes in Economics and Management Science** and **Information Systems** can be submitted between the **beginning of May and 15 July**. Applications for our **Teaching Degree Subject Area Business and Administration** can be submitted between the **beginning of May and 15 September**.

The application for our Bachelor’s degree programme in **Economics and Management Science** and **Information Systems** is submitted in a multi-stage process:

- Registration for the so-called dialogue-oriented service procedure (DoSV) in the [DoSV applicant portal](https://dosv.hochschulstart.de/ "Zum DoSV-Bewerberportal der Stiftung für Hochschulzulassung (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)") of the Foundation for University Admission. With your registration you will receive an applicant ID (applicant identification number) and a BAN (applicant authentication number).
- Application via the [AlmaWeb](https://almaweb.uni-leipzig.de/ "Zum Studienportal AlmaWeb (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)") study portal of Leipzig University whilst stating the applicant ID and the BAN from the registration with the DoSV.

You can find more information about the dialogue-oriented service procedure on the homepage of our [Student Secretariat](https://www.uni-leipzig.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung/zulassungsbeschraenkungen/#c4844 "Zur Homepage des Studierendensekretariates (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)").

The application for our **Teaching Degree Subject Area Business and Administration** is submitted in a single-stage process:

- Application via the [AlmaWeb](https://almaweb.uni-leipzig.de/ "Zum Studienportal AlmaWeb (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)") study portal of Leipzig University.

You can obtain further information on the application process (ranging from the application to admission and to enrolment) on the homepage our [Student Secretariat](https://www.uni-leipzig.de/studium/vor-dem-studium/bewerbung/online-bewerbung/ "Zur Homepage des Studierendensekretariates (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)").

### [Application for the first semester with a foreign HEEQ](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/study-programmes/applications\#collapse398685)

You have a **foreign education qualification** and have not yet taken examinations in a similar Bachelor’s degree programme?

Applications for our **Bachelor Degree** **Economics and Management Science** and **Information Systems** can be made between **15 April and 15 July** via the [uni-assist](https://www.uni-assist.de/bewerben/ "Zum Bewerbungsportal uni-assist (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)") application portal. Applications for our **Teaching Degree Subject Area Business and Administration** can be made between **15 April and 1 September** via the [uni-assist](https://www.uni-assist.de/bewerben/ "Zum Bewerbungsportal uni-assist (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)") application portal.

You can obtain further information on the application process (ranging from the application to admission and to enrolment) on the homepage our [International Centre](https://www.uni-leipzig.de/en/international/studying-at-leipzig-university/prospective-students/bachelors-state-examination-diplom "Zur Homepage der Stabstelle Internationales (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)").

### [Application for that a higher semester with a German HEEQ](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/study-programmes/applications\#collapse398708)

This option applies to those prospective students who have a **German HEEQ** (usually a German Abitur) and have already taken examinations in a similar Bachelor's degree programme.

It is possible to apply for the higher subject semester if you already have credits that can be recognised for the respective degree programme (usually credits that are similar in content and scope to the credits in the modules of our degree programmes) from a study programme at another university or college in a similar degree programme.

Please note that at the time of application for the higher subject semester and the associated application for placement in a higher subject semester, you must already have recognised achievements (as a rule, completed and assessed examination achievements). You can obtain further information on the recognition of achievements at the Faculty of Economics and Management Science [here](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/organisation-of-studies/recognition-of-achievements "Zu den Informationen zur Anerkennung von Leistungen (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)").

The application is submitted via the [AlmaWeb](https://almaweb.uni-leipzig.de/ "Zum Studienportal AlmaWeb (Link öffnet in einem neuen Tab)") study portal. ### [Information on the allocation of places in the Master's in Management Science](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/degree-programme/study-programmes/applications\#collapse402168)

Experience shows that the number of applicants exceeds the number of available study places in the M. Sc. degree programme in Management Science many times over. The Faculty of Economics and Management Science strives to attract excellent and committed students to the degree programme. The allocation of study places takes place through a multi-stage procedure.

**1\. Level: Application phase**

You can apply for a study place during the application period via the AlmaWeb study portal or the uni-assist application portal (please note the respective information in the previous tabs); **all** documents must be **completely** **submitted (online)** in the AlmaWeb or uni-assist portals by the end of the application deadline **at the latest**.

The following documents must be attached to your application:

- Letter of motivation;
- Curriculum vitae in tabular form;
- Proof of knowledge of the English language (B 2) - this proof can also be provided via the higher education entrance qualification (as a rule the Abitur), if this confirms at least seven years of school instruction in English up to graduation;
- for foreign applicants: proof of knowledge of the German language (C 1);
- Certificate of the first university degree (or a current overview of grades of the completed modules confirmed by the university, the sum of which must generally be at least 120 CP) - this must be a university degree with predominantly management science and economic content;
- if applicable, work and/or internship certificates.

Please check the application for completeness and correctness before sending it; **it is not possible to make subsequent changes or submit additional documents during the application and selection process!**

Please note that exceeding the application deadline or incomplete application documents will lead to exclusion from the selection procedure.

**2\. Level: Examination of professional suitability/admission requirements**

Immediately following the conclusion of the application period on 31 May, the review of the submitted application documents begins, on the basis of which it is examined whether the applicants meet the necessary professional requirements that allow them to expect successful participation in the degree programme. According to Section 2 of the study regulations, a first vocational university degree with predominantly management science as well as economic contents or a comparable degree (especially in the field of industrial engineering) is required.

The result of the examination of the professional suitability is sent to the applicant by means of a notification via the AlmaWeb study portal after the selection procedure has been completed.

**3\. Level: Selection Procedure**

Those applicants who have the professional suitability take part in the selection procedure for the allocation of study places.

Students are usually selected through selection interviews of the applicants with the members of a selection committee. The most promising applicants are invited (the criteria include: previous grades, CV, motivation, etc.).

The selection interviews are expected to take place between mid-June and mid-July. Invitations will be sent out at least a week in advance. Failure to attend the interview will be considered a withdrawal of the application. Due to the large number of selection interviews and the tight time frame, it is unfortunately not usually possible to reschedule appointments.

Up to a maximum of 50 percent of the study places can be awarded according to the grades of the proven module examinations of the first relevant vocational degree programme.

After the selection procedure has been completed, the admission and rejection notifications are sent via the AlmaWeb study portal. The sending of the notifications is expected to start in mid-July. Please note that the process will take some time.

**We hope you understand that the faculty cannot provide any information on the status of your application during the entire procedure; you can only view your current application status via the application portal.**
