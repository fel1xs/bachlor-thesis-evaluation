The goal of our research is to understand and analyse entrepreneurial activities in a competitive global environment as an important aspect of strategic business decisions.

[![enlarge the image: A facility and building of Leipzig University](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/4/4/csm_Internationalization_d52c225d24.jpg)](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/fileadmin/_processed_/4/4/csm_Internationalization_eadb4ddad0.jpg) ISC Program, Photo: Leipzig University

## Focus

We focus on questions concerning the actions of SMEs, such as the logic and benefit of the international activities of a company, the dominance of strategic options, and the specific success factors.

A further research focus of the ISC program lies in analysing the interaction between local institutions and international companies. The results from our research aim to show ways in which international company formations can be supported.
