iN4iN offers training and coaching solutions for representatives from organizations (companies, universities, NGOs, etc.) as well as business consultants in their home country and/or in Germany. All of our training comes with an official certificate supported by the prestigious Leipzig University. We can provide our trainings in German, English, Spanish, and Arabic.

## Trainings Solutions

### [Certified Innovation Manager Training](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/in4in/training\#collapse401240)

**Course Description:**

Our most sought-after program introduces various tools and techniques to prompt and manage innovation in an enterprise. The participants will immerse themselves in the innovation concept and will have the chance to work with innovation management tools and get to know how to apply them in different contexts., in hand with the professional coaching from our team of iN4iN instructors. This will ensure a successful outcome, as the participants will have comprehended the methods thoroughly, as well as be able to apply them in their immediate context.

**Duration**

2 weeks (50 training hours)

**Course content:**

- Innovation Strategy
- Understanding Customers
- Creativity Promotion
- Systematic Analysis of Opportunities
- Learning from Competitors
- Innovation Project
- Innovation Culture
- Innovation Process Management
- Intellectual Property
- Innovation Plan

### [Certified Market-Driven Innovation Manager](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/in4in/training\#collapse401242)

**Course Description:**

We also offer training solutions for those clients who want to learn the most important tools for the management of the innovation process inside a shorter modality. In this sense, the Market-driven Innovation Management program combines the first part of the Certified Innovation Manager program into a more intense modality. In this course, the participants will get acquainted with the innovation concept and a wide range of its tools and techniques, through an intensive learning environment.

**Duration:**

1 week (24 training hours)

**Course content:**

- Innovation Concept
- Understanding Customers
- Creativity
- Systematic Analisis of Opportunities
- Learning from Competitors
- New Product Business Model

### [Certified Innovation Management Coach](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/in4in/training\#collapse401244)

**Course Description:**

Our program is based on a “train the trainer” modality, upon which we certify consultants who have successfully completed our training and have proven to be effective innovation plan implementers and coaches.

The certification can be renewed every three years, upon fulfilling a set of requirements and covering a renewal fee.

**Duration**

2 weeks (50 training hours)

**This certification will help you to:**

- Achieve competitive prestige
- Increase your professional credibility
- Obtain new business opportunities
- Sustain market-driven innovation practices
- Prove your commitment to high-level innovation practices to your clients and stakeholders
- Increase your earning potential

### [Entrepreneurship Summer School](https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/sept-competence-center/in4in/training\#collapse402934)

**Course Description:**

Our Entrepreneurship Summer School approached is based on the experience with the EU MELES Project and the SMILE Founder Inititiative of Leipzig University, where together with other European partner universities, we provide an executive entrepreneurial experience.

**Duration:**

1 week (25 training hours)

**Course content:**

- Present a Business Idea
- Business Model Canvas
- Meet an Entrepreneur
- Design Thinking
- Understanding Customers
- Value Proposition
- 10 types of Innovation
- Key Resources and Partners
- Profit Model
- Pitching of the final idea
- Awarding
