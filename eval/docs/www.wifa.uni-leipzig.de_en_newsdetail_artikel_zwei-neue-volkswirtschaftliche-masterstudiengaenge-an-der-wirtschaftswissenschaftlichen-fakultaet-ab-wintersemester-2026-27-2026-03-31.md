News from  31/03/2026

https://www.wifa.uni-leipzig.de/en/newsdetail/artikel/zwei-neue-volkswirtschaftliche-masterstudiengaenge-an-der-wirtschaftswissenschaftlichen-fakultaet-ab-wintersemester-2026-27-2026-03-31

Starting in winter semester 2026/27, the Faculty of Economics and Management Science at Leipzig University will introduce two new English-language master’s programmes: MSc Economics and MSc Quantitative Economics for Sustainability (QuESt). These programmes will replace the previous master’s programme Volkswirtschaftslehre (Economics).

The [**MSc Economics**](https://www.uni-leipzig.de/en/studying/prospective-students/courses-of-study/degree-programme/course/show/economics-msc-1 "Link opens new tab") programme provides rigorous training in economic theory and quantitative methods. It focuses on the analysis of current economic issues and the study of economic policy. Students can specialise in **Economic Policy** or **Quantitative Economics**. The **Economic Policy** track covers applied topics in economic policy, while the **Quantitative Economics track** emphasizes advanced research methods in empirical and theoretical economics at the doctoral level.

The [**MSc Quantitative Economics for Sustainability (QuESt)**](https://www.uni-leipzig.de/en/studying/prospective-students/courses-of-study/degree-programme/course/show/quantitative-economics-for-sustainability-msc "Link opens new tab")  programme focuses on sustainability-related economic challenges. QuESt aims at solving questions related to the sustainability transformation of the economy, using quantitative methods from economics and the natural sciences. The programme places particular emphasis on data-driven analysis as well as economic and integrated economic and natural science modelling approaches.

With these two new programmes, the Faculty is establishing new areas of focus in research and teaching. Further information will be published in the coming weeks.
